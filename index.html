RingMeFor

Help and support

A real alarm for the messages you can't miss, even on silent or in a Focus.

For iPhone, with iOS 27 or later.

Set up

  1. Open RingMeFor and follow the setup: it explains the alarms, iOS asks whether RingMeFor may set alarms, and you pick your apps.
  2. Tap Connect next to an app, then Add the RingMeFor shortcut, then Add Shortcut. One shortcut covers all your apps.
  3. Shortcuts then shows All Shortcuts. Tap ‹ at the top left to go back to Library, tap Automation, and turn on the switch next to “When I get a notification from…”. Shared shortcuts arrive switched off.
  4. Go back to RingMeFor and tap Open the RingMeFor shortcut. Tap ⓘ at the bottom, then Privacy, and turn on Allow Running When Locked, so it works while your phone is locked.
  5. While you're in the shortcut, check that your app is listed after “When I receive a notification from”. If it isn't, tap + and pick it.
  6. In RingMeFor, open Settings → Test with the Phone Locked to check that it all works.

There's no need to run the shortcut yourself with ▶. It runs by itself when a notification arrives.

If it didn't ring

In RingMeFor, open the app and tap Why Didn't It Ring?. It checks everything RingMeFor can see, and lists what to look at. The usual causes:

  • The automation is switched off: Shortcuts → Library → Automation.
  • Allow Running When Locked is off, so nothing rings while the phone is locked.
  • A Focus, such as Sleep, holds back the app's notifications, or the app is in Scheduled Summary. RingMeFor only hears notifications your iPhone delivers, so allow the app in each Focus you use (Settings → Focus).
  • RingMeFor is turned off, or it's the free version, which rings for one app.
  • No rule matched. On the app's page, Test a Message shows what a message would do.
  • Your ringer volume is low. Alarms play at your ringer volume (Settings → Sounds & Haptics).

RingMeFor Pro

A one-time purchase that unlocks every app and every option. On a new iPhone, or after reinstalling, tap Settings → Restore Purchases. Apple handles payments and refunds: to ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Removing RingMeFor

Delete the RingMeFor shortcut in Shortcuts too, so its automation stops running.
