RingMeFor

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 7, 2026

RingMeFor doesn't collect any of your data. It has no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Everything it handles stays on your iPhone.

What RingMeFor handles, on your iPhone

  • Notifications from the apps in your RingMeFor shortcut. The Shortcuts automation you add passes RingMeFor each notification's app, title, subtitle, sender and text, when it was sent, and whether it's Time Sensitive. RingMeFor checks it against your rules, on your iPhone.
  • Notifications from apps you haven't added in RingMeFor are ignored and not kept. If you add an app RingMeFor doesn't know to the shortcut yourself, RingMeFor adds it to your apps, with no rules.
  • What it keeps: your apps, rules and settings; a History of the notifications it checks, with up to 140 characters of each message and up to 80 of its title, subtitle and sender, when it arrived, and what RingMeFor did; and when it last heard from the shortcut and passed a self-test, so it can tell you if something stops working. History keeps at most 300 entries, and older ones are removed.
  • Alarms show the app and who the notification is from, and the message too if you turn on Show the message. They appear on your Lock Screen and a paired Apple Watch, even if you hide notification previews.
  • Self-tests: if you use them, RingMeFor schedules its own notifications on your iPhone.

What leaves your iPhone

Nothing, from RingMeFor. It sends nothing to its developer or anyone else, and contains no third-party code that does. Apple's own services work as usual:

  • Backups: if you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer, RingMeFor's data is included, like other apps' data.
  • Crash reports: if you turn on Share With App Developers (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share crash reports with the developer. They don't include your notifications or History.
  • Purchases: RingMeFor Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment, and the developer never sees your payment details. RingMeFor checks the purchase on your iPhone with Apple's StoreKit.
  • Siri: if you ask Siri to turn RingMeFor on or off, Apple handles the request under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

  • To clear History: in RingMeFor, tap History, then the ••• button, then Clear history.
  • To delete an app or a rule, remove it in RingMeFor.
  • To delete everything, delete RingMeFor. Delete the RingMeFor shortcut in Shortcuts too, so its automation stops running.

The developer has no copy of your data, so what you delete can't be recovered, except from your own backups.

Children

RingMeFor isn't directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone.
